# Software Engineering Reports & Case Studies

Academic and technical reports authored during my BSc Computer Science studies at the University of the Western Cape (UWC)[cite: 5, 7], covering high-integrity software quality, reliability engineering, the history of the Software Crisis, and the evolution of modern software engineering practices[cite: 5, 7].

---

## 📄 Reports in This Repository

### 1. The Software Quality Antidote: Prescribing Rigor for Ailing Systems
- **File:** [`software-quality-antidote.pdf`](./software-quality-antidote.pdf)[cite: 5]
- **Core Themes:** Software quality frameworks (referencing **ISO/IEC 25010**), **Reliability** in safety-critical domains (nuclear energy, aerospace, medical devices), fault tolerance, robust architecture (safety monitors, heartbeat mechanisms), static analysis, fault injection, and **HAZOP**[cite: 5].
- **Case Study — Halden Reactor DI&C Project:** Examines the transition from analogue instrumentation and control to a modern **Digital Instrumentation and Control (DI&C)** system in a nuclear research setting—highlighting **IEC 60880** compliance, formal methods/specifications, V-Model verification and validation (V&V), and independent V&V teams to avoid confirmation bias[cite: 5, 6].

### 2. A Panoramic View of the Software Crisis and Its Lasting Impact
- **File:** [`software-crisis-panoramic-view.pdf`](./software-crisis-panoramic-view.pdf)[cite: 7]
- **Core Themes:** The gap between exponential hardware growth (Moore's Law / integrated circuits and the "tyranny of numbers") and early software delivery, ad-hoc **"Code and Fix"** development, cost/schedule estimation challenges, and the formalization of Software Engineering around the **1968 NATO conference**[cite: 7].
- **Methodologies & Modern Context:** Covers the **Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)**, structured programming (referencing **Edsger W. Dijkstra**), formal management and estimation models (such as **COCOMO**, Gantt charts, PERT, and CASE tools), **CHAOS report** project outcomes, and how **Agile** responds to ongoing requirements management and communication challenges[cite: 7, 8].
